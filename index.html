How to fold

8 pages · 1 sheet · 1 cut

  1. 1 Design your zine on one sheet, with the pages in this order and orientation.
  2. 2 Crease the sheet along both main directions — fold in half each way, then open flat.
  3. 3 Make two more creases to divide the long direction into quarters, then open flat again.
  4. 4 Fold in half, then cut along the center crease — through both layers, but only as far as the quarter-fold marks.
  5. 5 Open it out flat. You'll see a short slit running through the center of the sheet.
  6. 6 Fold in half along the long direction so all your pages are still on the outside.
  7. 7 Push inward from both short ends — the slit opens into a cross or diamond shape. Bring the pages together with the front cover outermost.
  8. 8 Press flat. That's your finished zine — 8 pages, no stapling or gluing needed!
